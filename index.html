2. Pricing

The external storage is what gets added when hosts are sized for compute and the rest of the storage goes outside vSAN. It's billed pay-as-you-go: commitments apply to hosts only.

3. Sizing assumptions (optional)

If a cluster is too small for the chosen policy, the tool falls back to RAID-1. HA spare hosts count toward compute capacity only. The free-space headroom is kept on both vSAN and external datastores.
