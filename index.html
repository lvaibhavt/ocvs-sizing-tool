Pricing & regions
Additional storage

Used only when it costs less than adding vSAN hosts (or is required, for shapes with no local vSAN). Cheapest picks the lowest-priced option that suits running VMs in the region.

Sizing assumptions

RAID-5 needs 4 hosts and RAID-6 needs 6; smaller clusters fall back to RAID-1. HA spares add compute only. Headroom is kept free on vSAN and on external datastores.
